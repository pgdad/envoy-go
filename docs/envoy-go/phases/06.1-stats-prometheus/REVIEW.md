## Strengths

- **Doctrine D-3.2 compliance is mechanical and grep-verifiable end-to-end.** Zero `prometheus/client_golang` or any other third-party stats/Prometheus library in `go.mod` / `go.sum`. The entire `internal/stats` package's external surface is the Go stdlib's eight imports. Future Observability-family phases (gRPC ALS, OTLP, statsd) can plug into `Registry.Walk` without converting through a Prometheus-shaped intermediate. ADR-0059 makes this commitment explicit and rejects three concrete alternatives.
- **Lock-free hot path with the `RWMutex` only at boot-time register sites.** `Counter.Inc` is a one-cycle `atomic.Uint64.Add`; `Gauge.{Inc,Dec,Set,Add}` are atomic-int64 ops; `Walk` holds `r.mu.RLock` for the duration of the iteration but contends with nothing on the hot path. The LBP-1 invariant — `Freeze` shuts the door on `NewCounter`/`NewGauge` — is what makes the read-path lock-free against concurrent Inc; once Frozen, `Walk` over a stable slice is data-race-free against atomic-load reads of each metric's value.
- **`checkFrozenLocked`'s atomic-load-inside-held-lock discipline closes a Freeze/NewCounter race that a naïve implementation would have introduced.** `registry.go:113-123` reads the `frozen` atomic INSIDE the Lock-acquired critical section, ensuring a Freeze that completes after a contending NewCounter's atomic-Load (but before its Lock acquisition) is still observed. The five-line comment explains the happens-before discipline (Freeze's Store-after-Lock-release pairs with Load-after-Lock-acquire). This is programmer-error-grade defense.
- **Rule SN4's empirical-pin discipline is exemplary.** The SPEC drafted phase paused at brainstorm-close to run reference Envoy v1.37.2 against a 5-request workload and copy the verbatim scrape lines into SPEC §10.1; the same evidence appears verbatim in ADR-0061 Context and in BEHAVIOR_CONTRACT lines 86-99 (no drift). The negative-confirmation grep `grep -E 'envoy_[a-z_]*_(1xx|2xx|3xx|4xx|5xx)'` returning 0 matches over the full 1181-line scrape closes the "did we get the suffix-stripping right?" question definitively.
