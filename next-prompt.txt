### ⚠️ WHAT THE SPEC DECIDED THAT THE PLAN MUST NOT RE-OPEN

1. **PER-HANDSHAKE `Clone()`, NEVER A PER-CHAIN PRE-BUILD** — and the reason is an **AUTHENTICATION BYPASS**, not performance. The install anchor is `internal/tls/config.go:57` (right after the `:53` `commonTLSContextToConfig` call and its `:54-56` error check), but **`cfg.ClientCAs` and `cfg.ClientAuth` are assigned LATER, at `:94-95`**, from the validation-context switch at `:99`.
