## What just landed (42.2a — the substrate 42.2b builds on)

`RetryPolicy.per_try_timeout` (field 3) — the project's FIRST request-scoped deadline (envoy-go does NOT implement the global `RouteAction.timeout`). The 42.1 `retryExecutorH1`/`retryExecutorH2` (`internal/filter/http/router/retry.go`) wrap each `do{H1,H2}ClusterAction` call in a child `context.WithTimeout(ctx, rp.perTryTimeout)`; on the child deadline expiring while the PARENT ctx is alive, the attempt is a per-try-timeout — a synthesized **504**, `IncUpstreamRqPerTryTimeout()`, retried via `perTryTimeoutRetriable()` (`matches(504,false) || retryReset`) under {5xx, gateway-error, retriable-status-codes∋504, **reset**}. The `reset`/`connect-failure` SPLIT (`retryReset` the 5th `retryOnBits` value) + the RACE-ROBUST discriminator (a wall-clock-deadline-elapsed fallback immune to context's timer-goroutine lag, scoped to the driver's synthesized-failure manifestation [`resp.localOrigin` H1 / `resp.localOrigin || resp.Status==0` H2], read before the explicit `cancel()`; the PARENT-alive guard preserves the H2 `Status:0` client-cancel-not-retried invariant). `upstream_rq_per_try_timeout` is the 6th `retryStats` member via `EnsureRetryStats`. Byte-identical when `per_try_timeout ≤ 0`. See ADR-0250 + `docs/envoy-go/phases/42.2-hedging/{SPEC,PLAN,PROGRESS}.md`.
