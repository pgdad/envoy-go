## What this is (phase 38 SPEC — what was pinned)

Phase 38 (`load-balancer-subset`) is the **FIFTH Load-balancing-family row** and the family's **FIRST wrapper**. `Cluster.lb_subset_config` (`envoy.config.cluster.v3`, proto field 22) is **NOT a `Cluster.LbPolicy` enum value** — it wraps the cluster's `lb_policy` child: it partitions the endpoint set into precomputed subsets (one per distinct `envoy.lb` endpoint-metadata value-tuple per selector) and delegates each subset to a child `loadBalancer` built from `lb_policy`. At pick time it resolves the route's `RouteAction.metadata_match["envoy.lb"]` to a subset and delegates; on a miss it applies the cluster-level fallback {NO_FALLBACK/ANY_ENDPOINT/DEFAULT_SUBSET}. The Load-balancing family STAYS OPEN — **3** candidates remain after 38 {locality-weighted LB, priority load balancing, panic thresholds} (all health-gated). The SPEC executed the §11 **D-SS1..D-SS7** empirical pins live this session (parallel-subagent fan-out against `envoyproxy/envoy:contrib-v1.37.2` @ `sha256:7edd5b0f…` + the v1.32.4 module cache + upstream v1.37.2 source) and was spec-document-reviewer APPROVED.
