- **success/error semantics** (SPEC §4.2 / §12.2) — single-server: `total` at dispatch; `success` on ANY decoded reply (incl. `-ERR`, `$-1`, `*-1`); `error` ONLY on a transport/pool failure. (Fragmented MGET/MSET reply-type accounting DEFERRED — proxied single-server.)
- **The 4 gauges** (SPEC §4.4 / §12.3) — only `downstream_cx_active` (Handle entry/exit) + `downstream_rq_active` (request received/reply sent) are filter-driven. The 2 `*_bytes_buffered` gauges are connection-buffer-managed upstream (`setConnectionStats`) → in envoy-go's synchronous single-flight terminal they stay exist-at-0 (coverage boundary). `downstream_cx_active` is the LIVE-mirrored gauge (held-open arm); the rest quiesced-zero.
- **The local-reply set** (SPEC §3.3 / §12.4) — ECHO (echoes arg as bulk; arity≠2→`splitter.invalid_request`) + QUIT (`+OK\r\n`+close) are deterministic differential arms; TIME (wall-clock) is NON-deterministic → local-reply UNIT-test-only (NOT a byte-equivalence arm); HELLO splits — `>2`-args→`-ERR HELLO options…not supported`, non-2/non-numeric proto (incl. `HELLO 3`)→`-NOPROTO unsupported protocol version`, BUT `HELLO 2`/no-arg PROXIED (it is a ClusterScopeCommand → `command.hello.*` + forwarded; NOT a differential arm). PING/AUTH/ECHO/TIME/QUIT emit NO `command.*` (singleton accessors outside the 180-table); HELLO error-paths emit no `command.hello.*` (return before the per-command total).
