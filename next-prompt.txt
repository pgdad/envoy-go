## What this is (phase 38 BRAINSTORM — what was chartered)

Phase 38 (`load-balancer-subset`) is the **FIFTH Load-balancing-family row** and the family's **FIRST wrapper**. `Cluster.lb_subset_config` (`envoy.config.cluster.v3`) is **NOT a `Cluster.LbPolicy` enum value** (unlike the five incumbents roundRobin/leastRequest/randomLB/ringHashLB/maglevLB) — it is a wrapper triggered by the presence of `lb_subset_config`, that selects a subset of endpoints matching the route's `metadata_match` and **delegates to the cluster's `lb_policy` as the child policy run within that subset**.
