## Two traps the SPEC must not walk into

- **`statsdrecv` is a DATAGRAM parser.** `ingest` (`test/helpers/statsdrecv/statsdrecv.go:131`) splits a UDP datagram on `\n`, where every line is complete BY CONSTRUCTION. A TCP `Read` carries no such guarantee — a line can span two reads. The new TCP listener must read line-at-a-time through `bufio` with a carried remainder and discard an incomplete trailing line at EOF. Per `reference_line_parser_extension_delimiter_reuse`, **trace one concrete split-read example byte-for-byte** before touching the parser.
- **The `bootstrap.go:556-561` ordering constraint survives the lift with INVERTED meaning.** `GetAddress()` returns nil for BOTH a missing oneof AND a `tcp_cluster_name` arm, which is why the reject ran FIRST. After the lift the `tcp_cluster_name` arm must be DISPATCHED before the nil-`address` reject fires, or a valid TCP config is rejected as "missing `statsd_specifier`". A regression test must pin this.
