## THE load-bearing finding (drives the leg)

A per-attempt-timeout (`RetryPolicy.per_try_timeout`, field 3) produces a synthesized **504** and is RETRIED iff `retry_on` ∈ {`5xx`, `gateway-error`, `retriable-status-codes`∋504, **`reset`**} — NOT `connect-failure`-alone (live-probed).
