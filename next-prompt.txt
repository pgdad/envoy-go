### ⚠️ THE DEFECT — A LIVE PRODUCTION DEADLOCK, REPRODUCED BY EXECUTION AT `f71cc7e2`

`internal/filter/hcm/h2/framer.go` wraps `http2.Framer.ReadFrame()` in a **read deadline and retries on timeout** — `(*framer).readFrameCtx` (50 ms, `:164`) and `(*framer).tryReadFrame` (1 ms, `:201`). `ReadFrame` reads via `io.ReadFull`, so **a deadline expiring MID-FRAME discards the bytes already drained off the socket**; the retry resumes at the wrong byte offset and the frame stream desynchronizes permanently. In the tiny-window workload (a long run of identical 13-byte `WINDOW_UPDATE` frames) one is silently swallowed, the writer parks forever in `(*window).reserveBlocking`, and the connection deadlocks with **NO error, NO GOAWAY and NO RST_STREAM**.

⚠️ **THREE PRODUCTION CALL SITES, NOT ONE:** `conn.go:259` (`(*ServerConn).Run`), `conn.go:304` (the batch-drain loop) and ⚠️ **`client.go:307` — the UPSTREAM client read loop, which the standing record OMITS ENTIRELY.**
