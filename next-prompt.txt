### ⚠️ THE CHARTER, AND THE DEFECT

**Remove the read-deadline polling from the HTTP/2 frame reader so a frame read is NEVER abandoned part-way.**

`internal/filter/hcm/h2/framer.go` wraps `http2.Framer.ReadFrame()` in a read deadline and retries on timeout — `readFrameCtx` (50 ms, `:170`) and `tryReadFrame` (1 ms, `:202`). `ReadFrame` reads via `io.ReadFull`, so **a deadline expiring MID-FRAME discards the bytes already drained off the socket**; the retry resumes at the wrong byte offset and the frame stream desynchronizes permanently.
