## Carried forward from the phase-56 BRAINSTORM — the two LOAD-BEARING seams

- **TRAILERS ARE STRUCTURALLY INVISIBLE to envoy-go's HTTP filters, and tap is the first feature whose PURPOSE collides with that.** `HttpBufferedTrace.Message` carries `trailers` as a first-class field (f3) and `MatchPredicate` has two dedicated trailer arms — but `FilterChain.RunDecodeTrailers` (`internal/filter/http/chain.go:454`) and `RunEncodeTrailers` (`:621`) have **ZERO non-test callers**, and HCM never dispatches them (`internal/filter/hcm/connection.go:565`; `internal/filter/hcm/h2dispatch.go:501-503` — the never-done "Task 18", written at phase 04). Response trailers are handled nowhere. **Phase 56 stays framework-zero-touch:** both trailer match arms are boot-rejected (ADR-0080), `Message.trailers` is NEVER populated, and the omission is a documented **COVERAGE BOUNDARY** in BEHAVIOR_CONTRACT. HCM trailer plumbing is deferred to its own future **framework-surgery row** (the `reference_close_direction_framework_gap` precedent). **Do not quietly widen this scope at the SPEC.**
- **A BUFFERED TRACE IS AN END-OF-STREAM ARTIFACT.** `http_response_headers_match` cannot resolve at decode time, so the predicate tree is evaluated **incrementally** and the assembled `TraceWrapper` is emitted at stream end **only once the match resolves** — never at `DecodeHeaders`. Corollary: `not_match`/`and_match` over a response arm needs a **TRI-STATE** per node (match / no-match / undetermined), not a bool. The exact mechanism (short-circuit? early-emit?) is a SPEC/PLAN question. This is the single most likely implementation error in the phase.
